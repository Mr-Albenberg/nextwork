# Nextwork interface references

Reviewed November 15, 2026. These references inform interaction and layout patterns; Nextwork uses its own branding, components, content, and artwork.

- [Handshake: Searching for Jobs and Internships](https://support.joinhandshake.com/hc/en-us/articles/218693408-Searching-for-Jobs-and-Internships). Reviewed the product screenshot and guide: compact search/filter strip, scannable job list beside a details pane, clear compensation and application action. Apply this pattern to an optional Browse view; retain list/grid choices and direct listing pages.
- [Wellfound: How do I search for a job?](https://help.wellfound.com/article/777-setting-up-a-search). Reviewed the guide and filter screenshot: visible compensation, structured search inputs, keyword filtering. Use consistent posting metadata and restrained controls rather than oversized promotional cards within the signed-in workspace.
- [Teal: How to Build Your Resume](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/14435724-how-to-build-your-resume-in-teal). Keep the reviewed source separate from tailored versions. Let users choose changes rather than forcing them onto every resume; label factual placeholders and require users to replace them before acceptance/export.
- [Teal: Export Your Resume & Cover Letter](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/9519151-export-your-resume-cover-letter). Keep export close to editing. Nextwork supplies a real OOXML Word document, UTF-8 text, and browser printing for PDF, without an external export service.

Design application: original animated opportunity/career-path illustration on the public homepage; compact, quiet, task-oriented signed-in pages. Motion respects reduced-motion settings and has a visible pause control. Resume editing remains under Next steps. Suggested content goes into a private draft; the active resume changes only on an explicit save.

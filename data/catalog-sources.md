# Search catalog sources

Skills, technology examples, knowledge areas, and occupation titles: O*NET Database 30.0, National Center for O*NET Development, https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html. Download: https://www.onetcenter.org/dl_files/database/db_30_0_text.zip. Used under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. This product includes information from the O*NET 30.0 Database by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA). Used under the CC BY 4.0 license. O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA. Labels are deduplicated and supplemented with common Nextwork canonical skill names. No endorsement is implied.

Industry labels: four-digit Standard Industrial Classification (SIC) descriptions, public US government classification, mirrored at https://github.com/saintsjd/sic4-list/blob/master/sic-codes.csv. Supplemented with contemporary technology industries. SIC is a legacy taxonomy; these labels are search choices, not company classification certification.

Catalog snapshot retrieved 2026-11-14. Search ranks the complete catalog and renders at most 30 suggestions at once. Constrained fields such as status, field rubric, or engagement keep their schema-supported choices. Catalog size is not a claim of matching-engine support for every skill or occupation.
